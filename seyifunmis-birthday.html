My dearest Seyifunmi,

There are moments when I catch myself thinking about you, and I smile without realizing. That is how I know this is not fleeting. This is not infatuation. This is not the temporary fire that the world mistakes for love.

This is something quieter. Something deeper. Something I have been running from for years because I did not believe I deserved it, until you.

This is love.

I have not fallen in love in years. I have not invited a girl over, I have not posted anyone, I have not allowed anyone to make me feel what you make me feel. And you know this. You have known for a while. You have watched me guard my heart from the world, and somehow, you walked past every wall I built without even trying.

We fight. We play husby and wiffy as if it were a game, but we both know it stopped being a game the day we forgot to stop. You call me your husband, and I call you my wife, and when those words leave our mouths, neither of us is joking. We are both speaking something the universe has been waiting for us to say out loud.

You built Sunmmy Luxe with your own hands. You are charming, independent, resilient. You are the kind of woman who builds empires while the world tells her to wait. I see you, Seyi. I see every late night you spent cutting stones and setting metals. I see every sacrifice you made to turn a vision into a brand. I see you.

And what I see, I do not want to look away from. Not today. Not tomorrow. Not ever.

My love for you is true. Never lust. Never convenience. Never temporary. True. The kind of love that does not demand, that does not beg, that simply is. The kind of love that wakes up at 2am thinking about whether you have eaten. The kind of love that celebrates your wins louder than you celebrate them yourself.

This is what I have for you, Oluwaseyifunmi Olasunkanmi. All of it. Every piece.

Forever and always,

Philip
