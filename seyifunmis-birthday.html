A Letter From Philip

Seyifunmi

With all that I am, Philip

My dearest Seyifunmi,

My love is in your heart, isn't it? You. You have fallen. But you are so stubborn. Speak up. Say how you truly feel. I know you have fallen. The way you look at me — that is a giveaway. You do not need to pretend. You are a baddie and you do not want to lose your steez, but you have already lost it.

But you know what — you are covered. Your secret is safe with me. I will not tell anyone. Tell the truth. What is the problem. Are you a gladiator. Fall in love. What is it?

Happy Birthday, Seyifunmi.

I wish you a very wonderful day. May God bless your new age. May He keep you happy always. May He give you the grace to keep soaring higher. You are a sweet person, and I am not the only one who sees it — but I am the one who refuses to stop looking.

You are tough. Not the kind of tough the world hands you. The kind you were born with. The kind that bends but does not break. The kind that has carried you through more than I will ever know, and still keeps you standing. That strength of yours — that is what I fell for. That is what holds me.

My love for you is true. Never lust. Never convenience. Never temporary. The kind that watches you fight your battles quietly. The kind that prays for you when you forget to pray for yourself. The kind that does not ask you to be soft, only to be yourself.

This is what I have for you, Oluwaseyifunmi. All of it. Every piece. Every prayer.

Forever and entirely,

Philip

Forever Isn't Long Enough

Seyifunmi, if you have read this far — thank you. Thank you for being the woman I think about at 2am. Thank you for letting me into your world, even when you were unsure.

I pray for you. I pray that this new year brings you closer to everything your heart has been quietly asking for. May God steady your steps. May He quiet the noise around you. May He give you peace in the places where you have been searching for it.

What you are going through right now is just a phase. It is not your story. It is not the end. I will watch you grow, and that will be one of the most fonding and beautiful things I have ever been allowed to witness. You will make it out of the dark hours, Seyi. I believe that with everything in me.

And I will be there. Through every season, every shift, every version of you that emerges. I will still care. I cannot help it, because I genuinely love you. More than words. More than pages. More than this letter can hold.

Yours, Always & Entirely

Philip
